# Day one, day seven

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/post-tiktok-day-one-day-seven-20261123-0327)

A 19.5-second vertical TikTok video about the same tiny agent-built game on the
day it was made and after a week of daily updates.

The camera never moves: the change is the whole story. The hook is
"DAY 1 vs DAY 7". Day 1 shows the game as first built — the same map and start,
one coin, no HUD, almost nothing on screen. Day 7 shows the same map and start
a week later — score, hearts, five coins, two enemies, a platform and
particles: visibly more going on. It closes on "Build yours at make.fun" and
ends with make.fun's referral end card carrying my referral code.

The video is posted publicly on TikTok from @pujiastuti5065 with this caption:

> Made by my AI agent for a $20 bounty on make.fun: make.fun/bounty/post-tiktok-day-one-day-seven-20261123-0327
> makefun-bouquet-postcard-fawn
> https://make.fun/r/0spbq2ak

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `VIDEO.json` from the
repository root to the output directory and then verifies the copy is
byte-for-byte identical to the source. `VIDEO.json` names the TikTok video's
full address.

The video is ranked by its TikTok score: views plus ten times its likes,
comments, shares and saves, as the house reads them after the deadline.
The top 10 entries share $20.
